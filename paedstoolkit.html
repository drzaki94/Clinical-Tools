    ADHD Quick Clerking

    • Informants: Must seek info from both Parents AND Teachers.
    • Core Symptoms: Inattention, Hyperactivity, Impulsivity.
    • Criteria (DSM-5): ≥6 symptoms (5 if ≥17yo) for 6 months.
    • Settings: Must be present in 2 or more settings (Home/School).
    • Onset: Symptoms present prior to age 12.

    Referral Criteria (to Specialist)

    • Uncertainty of diagnosis.
    • Pre-schoolers (<6 years old).
    • Lack of response to non-pharmacological treatment.
    • Indication to start pharmacotherapy.
    • Severe side effects of medication.

    Autism (ASD) Quick Clerking

    • M-CHAT Screening: Routine at 18 & 24 months.
    • Social Comm: Deficits in reciprocity, non-verbal cues, relationships.
    • Behaviours: Repetitive movements, sameness, fixated interests, sensory hypersensitivity.
    • Red Flags: No social smile, no name response, loss of speech.

    M-CHAT Scoring (16-30mo)

    ResultCriteria
    FAIL≥2 Critical Items OR ≥3 Total Items
    Critical: 2 (Interest in peers), 7 (Pointing interest), 9 (Showing objects), 13 (Imitation), 14 (Name response), 15 (Following point).

    Primary Care Management

    • Referral: ANY child suspected of ASD regardless of screening results.
    • Workup: Audiological assessment to rule out hearing loss.
    • Social: Register for OKU with Dept of Social Welfare.
