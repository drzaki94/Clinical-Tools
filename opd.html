Methadone Dosing Guide

⚠️ CAUTION: Start Low, Go Slow

Methadone accumulation occurs over 3-5 days. Deaths occur in the first 2 weeks due to over-rapid titration.

Induction (Day 1-3)

  • Initial Dose: 10–30 mg. (Use 10-20mg if low tolerance or elderly).
  • Max Day 1: Usually 30mg.
  • Increments: No more than 5–10 mg every 3–5 days.
Hepatitis B & C Management (MOH CPG)
NOTIFICATION MANDATORY within 7 days (Act 342).

1. Screening & Diagnosis

  • Screen: HBsAg (RDT or Lab). Screen high risk (Family, Partners, PWID, HCW, Pregnant).
  • Confirm: If HBsAg (+), check HBeAg, Anti-HBe, HBV DNA, ALT, FBC, LFT, INR, AFP.
  • Coinfection: Screen for HIV and HCV.

2. Treatment Initiation (Primary Care)

CIRRHOSIS
TREAT ALL if DNA detectable.
NON-CIRRHOTIC
Treat if ALT > 2x ULN AND:
• HBeAg(+): DNA >20,000 IU/ml
• HBeAg(-): DNA >2,000 IU/ml

3. Management

  • First Line Drugs: TDF (Tenofovir), ETV (Entecavir), TAF.
  • Target: Undetectable DNA. (HBsAg loss is optimal but rare).
  • Monitoring (Untreated): ALT, DNA, Fibrosis assessment every 6 months.
  • Pregnancy: Treat if DNA >200,000 IU/ml at 28-32 weeks (TDF preferred). NB: Vaccine + HBIG within 12-24h.

1. Screening & Diagnosis

  • Screen: Anti-HCV Antibody (RDT or Lab).
  • Confirm: HCV RNA (preferred) or HCV Core Antigen.
  • Assess: FBC, LFT, RP, INR, Genotype (if not pangenotypic DAA), HIV, HBsAg.

2. Fibrosis Staging (Crucial)

Use APRI or FIB-4 (Calc below) to rule out cirrhosis.
• APRI ≥ 1.5 or FIB-4 ≥ 3.25 = Likely Cirrhosis (Refer).
• APRI < 1.5 = Non-cirrhotic (Treat in Primary Care).

3. Management (Treat All)

  • Goal: Cure (SVR12 - Undetectable RNA 12 weeks post-tx).
  • Regimen (Common): Sofosbuvir + Daclatasvir (12 weeks). Pan-genotypic.
  • Refer If: Cirrhosis, Renal failure (eGFR<30), Pregnancy, HIV/HBV coinfection, Treatment Failure.

Intervention Strategy (MOH CPG)

The 5 A's (Routine Intervention)

  • Ask: Document smoking status for every patient at every visit.
  • Advise: Clear, strong, personalized advice to quit.
  • Assess: Willingness to make a quit attempt now?
  • Assist: Counselling + Pharmacotherapy. Set Quit Date (STAR).
  • Arrange: Follow-up (1st week, then monthly).

The 5 R's (Unwilling to Quit)

Relevance: Why quitting matters personally.
Risks: Acute (SOB), Long-term (Ca), Environmental.
Rewards: Health, money, taste, smell.
Roadblocks: Identify barriers (withdrawal, stress).
Repetition: Repeat intervention every visit.

Behavioural Modification Principles

  • STAR Method (Preparation):
    • Set a quit date (within 2 weeks).
    • Tell family/friends for support.
    • Anticipate challenges (withdrawal).
    • Remove tobacco products from environment.
  • The 3 A's (Triggers): Address Accessibility, Availability, and Anonymity of the habit.
  • D.E.A.D. (Coping): Delay, Escape, Avoid, Distract.
Pharmacotherapy Reference
Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT)
Patch (24h/16h):

≥10 cigs/day: Start Step 1 (21mg/25mg) x 6-8 weeks, then taper.
<10 cigs/day: Start Step 2 (14mg/15mg).
Tip: Rotate site daily. Remove at night if insomnia occurs.

Gum (2mg/4mg):

Use 4mg if highly dependent/smoke >20/day. Otherwise 2mg.
Technique: "Chew & Park" (Chew until peppery taste, park between cheek/gum, repeat). Avoid acidic drinks 15m before.

Combination Therapy: Patch (Basal) + Gum/Inhaler (Bolus) is more effective than monotherapy.
Non-Nicotine Medications
Varenicline (Champix):

Start: 1-2 weeks BEFORE quit date.
Dose: 0.5mg OD (Days 1-3) -> 0.5mg BD (Days 4-7) -> 1mg BD (Day 8 onwards) x 12 weeks.
SE: Nausea (take with food), abnormal dreams.

Bupropion SR:

Start: 1-2 weeks BEFORE quit date.
Dose: 150mg OD x 3 days, then 150mg BD x 7-12 weeks.
Contraindication: Seizure history/risk, Eating disorders, MAOI use.

EVALI & Complications

EVALI Management (MOH CPG 2021)

Definition: E-cigarette/Vaping Product Use-Associated Lung Injury.

Diagnosis of Exclusion:
  1. History of e-cig/vape use (90 days).
  2. Pulmonary infiltrates on CXR/CT (Ground glass, consolidation).
  3. Absence of pulmonary infection (Workup: Viral panel, Influenza, Covid, Bacterial culture).
Admission Criteria: SpO2 <95%, Respiratory distress, Co-morbidities.
Treatment:
  • Supplemental Oxygen.
  • Systemic Corticosteroids (e.g., Methylprednisolone 0.5-1mg/kg).
  • Antibiotics (Empiric, until infection ruled out).

General Smoking Complications

Cardiovascular: IHD, Stroke, PVD.
Respiratory: COPD, Lung Ca, Asthma exacerbations.
Reproductive: Infertility, Low birth weight, SIDS.
Others: Periodontitis, Osteoporosis, Surgical complications.

DSM-5 MDD Criteria

5+ symptoms during same 2-week period (Must include #1 or #2):

  1. Depressed mood most of the day.
  2. Markedly diminished interest/pleasure (Anhedonia).
  3. Significant weight loss/gain or appetite change.
  4. Insomnia or hypersomnia.
  5. Psychomotor agitation or retardation.
  6. Fatigue or loss of energy.
  7. Feelings of worthlessness or excessive guilt.
  8. Diminished ability to think/concentrate.
  9. Recurrent thoughts of death/suicide.

DSM-5 GAD Criteria

Excessive anxiety/worry (more days than not for 6 months). Difficult to control worry. Associated with 3+ of:

  1. Restlessness or feeling keyed up/on edge.
  2. Being easily fatigued.
  3. Difficulty concentrating or mind going blank.
  4. Irritability.
  5. Muscle tension.
  6. Sleep disturbance.
