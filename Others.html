The ASSIST is designed to be administered by a health professional as part of a verbal interview with an adult patient, or self-administered electronically. "These substances can be smoked, swallowed, snorted, inhaled, injected or taken in the form of pills."

ASSIST direka untuk dikendalikan oleh profesional kesihatan sebagai sebahagian daripada temu bual lisan dengan pesakit dewasa, atau ditadbir sendiri secara elektronik. "Bahan-bahan ini boleh dihisap, ditelan, disedut, disuntik atau diambil dalam bentuk pil."

Instructions for Q2-Q5: Ask these questions for EACH substance the patient selected "Yes" to in Question 1. Calculate the score below for the specific substance being evaluated.

Arahan untuk S2-S5: Tanya soalan-soalan ini untuk SETIAP bahan yang pesakit tandakan "Ya" pada Soalan 1. Kira skor di bawah untuk bahan spesifik yang sedang dinilai.

Q2. In the past three months, how often have you used the substances?

S2. Dalam tiga bulan lepas, berapa kerap anda menggunakan bahan tersebut?

Q3. During the past 3 months, how often have you had a strong desire or urge to use?

S3. Dalam 3 bulan lepas, berapa kerap anda mempunyai keinginan kuat untuk menggunakannya?

Q4. During the past 3 months, how often has your use led to health, social, legal or financial problems?

S4. Dalam 3 bulan lepas, berapa kerap penggunaan anda menyebabkan masalah kesihatan, sosial, undang-undang/kewangan?

Q5. During the past 3 months, how often have you failed to do what was normally expected of you because of your use?

S5. Dalam 3 bulan lepas, berapa kerap anda gagal melakukan apa yang diharapkan kerana penggunaan anda?

Lifetime Impact Questions

Soalan Impak Sepanjang Hayat

Q6. Has a friend or relative or anyone else ever expressed concern about your use?

S6. Pernahkah rakan atau saudara mara menyatakan kebimbangan tentang penggunaan anda?

Q7. Have you ever tried and failed to control, cut down or stop using?

S7. Pernahkah anda cuba dan gagal untuk mengawal, mengurangkan atau berhenti menggunakannya?

Q8. Injection Drug Use

S8. Penggunaan Dadah Suntikan

Have you ever used any drug by injection? (NON-MEDICAL USE ONLY)

Pernahkah anda menggunakan sebarang dadah melalui suntikan? (BUKAN UNTUK TUJUAN PERUBATAN)

If "Yes, in the past 3 months", ask the extra drug injection questions:

Jika "Ya, dalam 3 bulan lepas", tanya soalan tambahan ini:

During the past three months, how often have you injected drugs? Dalam 3 bulan lepas, berapa kerap anda menyuntik dadah?
Have you ever injected drugs three or more days in a row? Pernahkah anda menyuntik dadah tiga hari berturut-turut atau lebih?

Injection Use Override

Pengecualian Suntikan

Patients who have injected drugs in the last three months, but no more than once per week or never more than three days in a row, should receive a brief intervention.

Pesakit yang menyuntik dadah dalam 3 bulan lepas (tidak melebihi sekali seminggu / tidak melebihi 3 hari berturut-turut) mesti menerima intervensi ringkas.

All other patients who have injected drugs in the last three months should receive a brief intervention that includes options for treatment.

Pesakit lain yang menyuntik dadah dalam 3 bulan lepas mesti menerima intervensi ringkas berserta pilihan rawatan.

The AUDIT-C is a 3-item alcohol screen that can help identify persons who are hazardous drinkers or have active alcohol use disorders (including alcohol abuse or dependence).

AUDIT-C ialah saringan alkohol 3 item yang boleh membantu mengenal pasti peminum berisiko atau mereka yang mempunyai gangguan penggunaan alkohol aktif.

Q1. How often do you have a drink containing alcohol?

S1. Berapa kerap anda minum minuman yang mengandungi alkohol?

Q2. How many standard drinks containing alcohol do you have on a typical day?

S2. Berapa banyak minuman standard yang mengandungi alkohol yang anda minum pada hari biasa?

Q3. How often do you have six or more drinks on one occasion?

S3. Berapa kerap anda mengambil enam atau lebih minuman pada satu-satu masa?

AUDIT-C Total Score

Jumlah Skor AUDIT-C

Sum of Q1, Q2, and Q3. Total score ranges from 0-12. Hasil tambah S1, S2, dan S3. Jumlah skor dari 0-12.
